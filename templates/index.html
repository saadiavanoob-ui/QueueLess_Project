10 Core Smart Features

Everything required for a national-level exhibition demonstration is fully implemented with real database backing.

1. QR Check-in

Dynamic QR Code generated for every token. Staff scan QR codes upon arrival to confirm attendance.

2. Multiple Counters

Multiple service desks (Counter 1, 2, 3...) run concurrently without duplicate token collisions.

3. Smart Wait Estimation

Calculates dynamic wait time: (people_ahead × avg_duration) ÷ active_counters.

4. Token Cancellation

Citizens can cancel tokens anytime from mobile. Automatically re-balances remaining wait times.

5. Feedback & Rating

Post-service 1–5 star ratings and reviews help administration monitor counter quality.

6. Multilingual UI

Instant toggle between English, Malayalam (മലയാളം), and Hindi (हिन्दी) with voice announcements.

7. Accessibility

High contrast mode, font size scalers (A, A+, A++), and screen-reader friendly design.

8. Public TV Display

High-visibility full-screen waiting room board with flashing alerts and live queue ticker.

9. Appointment Booking

Choose between instant walk-in token or schedule specific time slot for today/tomorrow.

10. Search Institution

Instant search by institution name, category, or branch location across all 8 sectors.
